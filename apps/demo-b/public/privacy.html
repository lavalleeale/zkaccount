Privacy policy

Effective August 25, 2026

zkAccount Demo B is an experimental, open-source application for using a smart account on the selected Base Sepolia or Ethereum Sepolia test network. This policy explains how the demo handles information when you create a passkey, authorize it, or use the blockchain features.

Information the demo accesses

  • Passkey authorization redirect. To authorize a new passkey, the demo sends its RP ID, public key, a return URL, a one-time state value, and the selected chain ID to a separate passkey manager origin, which is the only site that performs Google authentication. The demo never receives or processes the Google ID token itself; it only reads the manager's redirect result (a status, the resolved account, and the authorized device address) to complete setup.
  • Passkey data. Your browser and authenticator create or retrieve a P-256 passkey. The demo stores its public credential ID, public-key coordinates, and relying-party hash locally, then asks the authenticator for a fresh user-verified WebAuthn assertion whenever you sign.
  • Blockchain data. The demo reads account addresses, balances, authorized device addresses, and transaction status from the selected Sepolia network. If you submit an operation, its smart-account address, device public address, proof and public inputs, and transaction data are sent to the configured RPC and bundler services and may be recorded publicly onchain.
  • WalletConnect data. When you pair with a dapp, the demo receives that dapp's connection and signing requests through WalletConnect's encrypted relay. Requests can contain the dapp origin, transaction details, and messages you are asked to sign.

How information is used and stored

The private key backing your passkey never leaves your authenticator, and Demo B never handles a Google ID token or the Google subject identifier. The one-time state value and the passkey's public metadata are held in session storage only until the authorization redirect returns, then deleted. Demo B stores the public smart-account address, configured factory, chain ID, public passkey metadata, and WalletConnect session data in browser storage so that a returning user can use the wallet. It does not operate an account database or store a device private key.

A passkey remains under the control of your browser, operating system, authenticator, and any passkey-sync provider you use. Public blockchain records are permanent and cannot be deleted by this demo.

Third parties and sharing

The demo does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information is shared only as needed for the features you request: the passkey manager origin performs Google authentication and proof generation for new device authorizations; browser and authenticator providers manage passkeys; WalletConnect relays encrypted dapp sessions and requests; hosting providers deliver the site; configured RPC and ERC-4337 bundler providers process blockchain requests; and the selected Sepolia network publishes submitted transactions. Those parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The demo does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers, or application cookies.

Your choices

You may use the site without authorizing a new passkey, stop before submitting an onchain operation, reject or disconnect a dapp session, revoke the local device, manage Google connections in your Google Account through the passkey manager, and manage passkeys or site storage through your browser or operating system. Onchain records already submitted cannot be erased.

Security and test-network notice

The demo minimizes disclosure by keeping credentials on your device and delegating Google authentication to a single dedicated origin, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Both supported Sepolia networks are public test networks. Do not use the demo with real funds or information you are unwilling to expose through public blockchain activity.

Children

The demo is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the demo evolves. Material updates will be reflected here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the zkAccount GitHub repository.
