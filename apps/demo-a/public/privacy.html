Privacy policy

Effective August 25, 2026

zkAccount Demo A is an experimental, open-source application for creating and using a smart account on the selected Base Sepolia or Ethereum Sepolia test network. This policy explains how the demo handles information when you use Google authentication, a passkey, or the blockchain features.

Information the demo accesses

  • Google authentication data. When you continue with Google, Google sends an ID token to your browser. The demo uses its issuer, audience, stable Google subject identifier, nonce, issued-at time, and expiration time to validate the login and create a zero-knowledge authorization proof. The token may contain other basic account claims supplied by Google, but the demo does not use them to create a profile.
  • Passkey data. Your browser and authenticator create or retrieve a P-256 passkey. The demo stores its public credential ID, public-key coordinates, and relying-party hash locally, then asks the authenticator for a fresh user-verified WebAuthn assertion whenever you sign.
  • Blockchain data. The demo reads account addresses, balances, authorized device addresses, approved Google client-ID hashes, and transaction status from Base Sepolia. If you submit an operation, its smart-account address, device public address, proof and public inputs, and transaction data are sent to the configured RPC and bundler services and may be recorded publicly onchain.

How information is used and stored

Google token processing and proof generation happen locally in your browser. The demo does not intentionally send the full Google ID token or the Google subject identifier to an application backend, and it does not operate an account database. Authentication results, proof data are held in page memory and disappear when the page is reloaded or closed. Public passkey metadata is retained in local storage so the credential can be used again. Demo A may display selected token claims in the page during that session for development transparency.

A passkey remains under the control of your browser, operating system, authenticator, and any passkey-sync provider you use. Public blockchain records are permanent and cannot be deleted by this demo. The zero-knowledge proof is designed to authorize the account without revealing the Google ID token or subject identifier onchain, but its public inputs and the resulting account activity remain public.

Third parties and sharing

The demo does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information is shared only as needed for the features you request: Google provides authentication; browser and authenticator providers manage passkeys; hosting providers deliver the site; configured RPC and ERC-4337 bundler providers process blockchain requests; and the selected Sepolia network publishes submitted transactions. Those parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The demo does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers, or application cookies.

Your choices

You may use the site without continuing with Google, stop before submitting an onchain operation, close or reload the page to clear session memory, manage Google connections in your Google Account, and manage passkeys through your browser or operating system. Onchain records already submitted cannot be erased.

Security and test-network notice

The demo minimizes disclosure by processing credentials locally and using a zero-knowledge proof, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Both supported Sepolia networks are public test networks. Do not use the demo with real funds or information you are unwilling to expose through public blockchain activity.

Children

The demo is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the demo evolves. Material updates will be reflected here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the zkAccount GitHub repository.
